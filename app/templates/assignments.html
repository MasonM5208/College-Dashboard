Checking for changes

Canvas is checked every 30 minutes. Canvas caches the feed on its own servers, so a change you make there can take up to an hour to appear here — treat anything due in the next two hours as needing a look at Canvas itself.

What this list cannot show

Only work that has a due date in Canvas appears here. Anything a professor assigns in class, on a paper syllabus, or without setting a Canvas due date is invisible to this feed, and courses that do not use Canvas this way will not appear at all. This list is a floor, not a full picture. Entering things by hand arrives with the next milestone.
