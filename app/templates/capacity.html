Coursework hours are the smaller of your daily budget and what is actually unbooked, with practice taken off the top. Everything on Today is ranked against these numbers.

Hours per day

Budget is how many hours of coursework you can really do on that day — not how many hours exist. Four is a normal figure and a good starting point.

Practice is protected. It is subtracted before any assignment is ranked, so a deadline can never quietly eat it. Practice has no due date, so in any ranking that sorts by urgency it would lose every time — which is exactly how a performance major finds out in October that they have not practised since August.
